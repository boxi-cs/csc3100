Data StructuresInteractive lecture notes

One site per lecture. Each one follows the lecture, lets you step through what each data structure does in memory, asks you to predict before it shows you, and ends with a self-check and exercises. Every Java program has been compiled and run, and every output shown is the real output.

Lectures 1–15 each have four videos of 8–12 minutes, with English and Chinese subtitles; they also appear inside each lecture, in the chapter they cover. Greyed-out lectures open later in the term.
